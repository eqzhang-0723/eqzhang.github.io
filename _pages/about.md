I am a third-year master’s student in the School of Geography and Ocean Science at Nanjing University, China. 

My primary research interests include food security, energy economics, spatial inequality, and applied microeconomics, with a focus on sub-Saharan Africa. My work examines how transport infrastructure shapes food access and economic development. Another strand of my research investigates how environmental factors influence food security, with particular attention to water and atmospheric conditions. Methodologically, I am interested in combining qualitative insights with quantitative analysis, and I work with spatial econometric models and geospatial/remote-sensing data.

I received my B.A. in Rural Development Studies from China Agricultural University in 2023, and I will receive my M.S. in Human Geography from Nanjing University in June 2026. I also completed a Minor in Economics at Peking University in 2023.

Since April 2025, I have been a Fellow at UNICEF Zimbabwe Country Office, working on child nutrition and early childhood development.
